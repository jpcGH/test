Our Story

A Love Written in Quiet Moments

We met on an ordinary Tuesday that somehow felt extraordinary. What began as a shared ride after work became long conversations, laughter that lingered, and a friendship that slowly softened into love.

Over the years, we learned each other in the small details — coffee just right, hand-holding in traffic, whispered prayers before big decisions, and the comfort of being fully seen. Our journey has been shaped by grace, family, and tradition.

This celebration is not one day, but three meaningful chapters: our legal vows, our cultural heritage, and our covenant before God. Thank you for being part of our story.
